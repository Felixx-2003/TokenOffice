# Token Circus — Stage 2

1. Press Space or crack the Prompt Whip to make every hired model perform together, generate Tokens, and build Audience Hype.
2. Recruit ten AI acts: Llama, Qwen, Mistral, DeepSeek, GLM, Kimi, Grok, Gemini, Claude, and ChatGPT.
3. Improve each act with ten Trick Levels and five costume tiers, from Cardboard to Questionably Expensive.
4. Fill the Hype Meter to trigger a twelve-second Standing Ovation worth triple output.
5. Build up to five Auto-Whip Cannons for idle prompting. Offline production is capped at eight hours.
6. Survive random circus incidents that temporarily multiply production.
7. End mature runs with a Curtain Call to earn permanent Golden Tickets and move through increasingly absurd venues.
8. Complete the stage by earning 25 Golden Tickets, building five Auto-Whip Cannons, dressing all performers in at least Legendary costumes, and firing the Great Token Cannon.
