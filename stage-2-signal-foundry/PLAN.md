# Signal Foundry — Stage 2

1. Tap the signal core or press Space to collect Flux and charge Resonance.
2. Buy eight escalating systems, from a Pocket Antenna to a Horizon Loom. Every owned system produces Flux automatically.
3. Calibration improves manual tuning, while Overclock doubles the whole fleet. Buy modes support 1, 10, or maximum purchases.
4. Filling Resonance starts a twelve-second 3× surge. Manual tuning is the active layer; the fleet and offline earnings are the idle layer.
5. At 1M run Flux, Fold the run into Echoes. Echoes permanently multiply all future output.
6. Complete the stage by reaching 25 Echoes, Overclock 8, and owning 10 Horizon Looms.
7. Keep the main loop visible at desktop sizes, support mobile play, local saves, offline earnings, keyboard play, reduced motion, and no live APIs.
