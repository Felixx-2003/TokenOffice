# Token Heist — Stage 3

1. Press Space or Run Heist to make the whole hired crew generate Context Coins and breach the vault.
2. Recruit ten AI specialists and improve their Gadgets and Disguises.
3. Fill Vault Breach to trigger a fifteen-second Perfect Heist worth four times production.
4. Buy Getaway Bots for idle production and up to eight hours of offline earnings.
5. Escape mature runs for permanent Master Blueprints and increasingly impossible targets.
6. Win with 30 Master Blueprints, six Getaway Bots, ten Legendary Mastermind disguises, and a successful Root Access crack.
